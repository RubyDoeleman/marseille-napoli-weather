# Marseille → Napoli race weather

Live weather along the Marseille–Napoli ultra cycling route (1,474 km): temperature, rain, wind speed and direction every 100 km, at every stage end and on the big summits — timed to your own start, speed and stage plan.

**Open it:** https://rubydoeleman.github.io/marseille-napoli-weather/

- Enter your race start, daily start time, average speed (incl. stops), number of stages and the length of each stage. Your plan is saved in your browser only.
- The forecast is fetched live from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) (CC BY 4.0) each time you open the page, and the last one is kept for when mobile signal is patchy.
- The "live ↗" link on each row opens Windy at that point.

Unofficial page, not affiliated with the race organisers. Route from Komoot tour 3167057471; place names © OpenStreetMap contributors.
